# Audio credits and licensing

All sound in CITY OF CHAOS is generated in code at runtime with the Web Audio API.

- **No audio files** are included or downloaded: no samples, no recordings, no third-party tracks, no CDN or online service.
- **Music** (`src/systems/Music.js`) is original: seven tracks (menu, exploring, driving, danger, police chase, combat, casino) composed for this game and synthesised live. The chord loops are ordinary generic harmony (minor-key loops, ii-V-I jazz turnarounds); the melodies, bass lines and drum patterns are original or randomly generated each time. No existing song, melody or recording is copied or imitated.
- **Sound effects and ambience** (`src/systems/Audio.js`) are synthesised from oscillators and filtered noise.
- Because everything is original code, **no attribution or third-party licence is required**; the audio is covered by the project's own licence (Jeyem).

If an audio file from another source is ever added, record its author, source URL and licence in this file first (prefer CC0), and do not use anything labelled only "no copyright".
